Policy

Prototypes exploring how planners write policy that is connected to evidence, consultation inputs and tracked over time. Pick one to try it out.

User need: As a planner, I need to write policy that is connected to evidence, consultation inputs and tracked over time.

Current prototypes

These are the most recent prototypes we've developed. We'll be testing these over the next few weeks.

Policy drafting with starting points

Turn the starting points for a new plan into chapters, give each a brief and officers, gather its sources and choose its policies, then draft them with the evidence and consultation responses open alongside.

View policy summary

Browse policies by policy area and see a summary of each one: what it says, the evidence behind it, what consultation responses said, and the team's notes.

Previous versions

These are prototypes we've tested through Design Feedback sessions and other informal tests.

Write policy with starting points pre-filled

Work through the starting points for a new policy, gather the sources behind it, and draft it — starting from realistic example data already filled in.

Write policy without starting points

The same journey, starting from a blank slate — add your own starting points, topics and policy content from scratch to experience how the journey works.

Policy drafting: side by side policy/evidence viewer

A second take on the drafting workspace, on a wider screen. Evidence and the policy draft sit side by side with a divider you can drag between them, sources come from a search of the evidence base, and an automatic check flags where the draft does not use the sources attached to it.
