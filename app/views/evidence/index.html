Evidence

Prototypes exploring how planners identify, gather, analyse and use evidence in the plan-making process. Pick one to try it out.

User need: As a planner, I need to identify, gather, analyse and use evidence in the plan-making process.

Current prototypes

These are the most recent prototypes we've developed. We'll be testing these over the next few weeks.

GIS layer library

All the spatial data for your plan in one place, as policy layers and evidence base layers, with a map preview, version and the consultation each was published with. Add layers from your GIS database or an external source and link them to policies. The library sits beside the evidence library and shares its layers with the policy writing starting points, commissioned evidence and policy drafting.

Managing commissioned evidence

Brief an external consultant, receive their report, review it with comments and notes, and accept it into the evidence library. Includes the consultant's own screen for submitting the report.

Review evidence

Review a document and its details, tag it by hand or from an automatic summary with suggested tags, and add tagged notes alongside it.
