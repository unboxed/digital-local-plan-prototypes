Viewing and structuring evidence

Exploring ideas for how officers can view evidence sources and connect specific insights to policies as they are drafting.

User need: As a policy officer, I need to pick out the insights in my evidence and connect them to the policies I'm drafting, so that every policy is clearly backed by its evidence.

These are linked screens showing the ideas. Buttons such as Save tags, Edit, Remove and Export don't do anything yet. The examples are all in the Offices policy area.

Start

Screens

  1. View a document: a source document with passages tagged as fact, expert opinion or concept.
  2. View a hearing transcript: the same, for what was said at a hearing, with inspector and member comments.
  3. View an evidence item: the insights drawn from a source, with its summary and key findings.
  4. View a list of evidence: evidence items gathered on a theme, ready to export or share.
  5. Draft a policy with evidence: a policy whose clauses each cite the evidence item behind them.
