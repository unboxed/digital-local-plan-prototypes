Managing commissioned evidence

Commission a piece of evidence from an external consultant and see it through to the evidence library. Write a brief and send it, receive the consultant's report, review it with comments and notes, then tag it and accept it as a source for policy writing.

This journey involves two people. Most screens are seen by a policy officer, Danny Dyer. One, submitting the report, is seen by the consultant, Elena Waters, who works outside the council. Panels marked Prototype only let you switch between the two where the work passes from one to the other.
