Examination - inspector view

This prototype explores what an independent planning inspector sees when examining a submitted local plan. Unlike the other prototypes, this view is for an external viewer rather than LPA staff, so it does not use the site's internal navigation.

User need: As a planning inspector, I need to examine a submitted local plan and its supporting evidence to assess whether it is sound and legally compliant.

Choose a chapter and policy from the sidebar to start reading the plan paragraph by paragraph, alongside the evidence, comments and national policy references related to it.
