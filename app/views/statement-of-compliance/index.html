Statement of compliance

A statement of compliance is a formal procedural document required under England's local plan-making system, demonstrating that a local planning authority has met the legal and procedural requirements of the Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026 during plan preparation.

User need: As a local planning authority, I need to show which legislative requirements I have met during plan preparation, and the evidence that justifies each one.

Draft statement of compliance

For use at Gateway 2 to check progress — not every requirement is met yet, and each requirement's evidence page shows the planned activities still to come.

Completed statement of compliance

Every requirement fully met, with no further action required — the statement as it would stand once the plan has been adopted.

General conformity with the spatial development strategy

The evidence for Regulation 28: every policy of the spatial development strategy, using the London Plan as the example, with the local plan policies it is linked to and the authority's comment on each. Where a policy is not addressed in the local plan, the comment links to evidence that shows it is unnecessary.

User need: As a local planning authority, I need to show how my local plan is in general conformity with the spatial development strategy, policy by policy, and justify any policy it doesn't address.
