Statement of compliance

A statement of compliance is one of the documents a local planning authority submits with its proposed local plan for independent examination. It shows how the plan meets the requirements prescribed for Gateway 3 by regulation 32 of the Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026 (opens in a new tab), and the evidence for each.

Draft statement of compliance

For checking progress before Gateway 3 — not every requirement is met yet, and each requirement's evidence page shows the planned activities still to come.

Completed statement of compliance

Every requirement fully met, with no further action required — the statement as it would be submitted with the plan for independent examination.

General conformity with the spatial development strategy

Part of the evidence for regulation 32(i), regard to the rest of the development plan: every policy of the spatial development strategy, using the London Plan as the example, with the local plan policies it is linked to and the authority's comment on each. Where a policy is not addressed in the local plan, the comment links to evidence that shows it is unnecessary.
