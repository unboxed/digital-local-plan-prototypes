Managing main modifications

During the hearings, the inspector and the council’s team suggest changes to the plan. The council captures each one, the policy manager assigns it to an officer, and the team drafts the modifications, keeping a tracked changes version of the plan and a schedule of every modification. It then consults on the main modifications for at least 6 weeks and sends the comments to the inspector.

Besides main modifications, needed to make the plan sound, the council can make additional modifications that don’t affect soundness, and minor changes such as typos and cross-references.

You are a policy officer, Priya Shah, at the City of London Corporation, working with the rest of the policy team. You will only see the council’s screens. Where the inspector or a consultee would do something, a panel marked Prototype only does it for you.

The prototype starts after the consultation, with examples to look at: a version of the plan published for consultation and the comments made on it, and the team revising some modifications in the light of them, with modifications in draft, waiting for review and agreed.
