Managing main modifications

After the hearings, the inspector tells the council which main modifications the plan needs to be sound. The council drafts them, keeps a schedule of every modification, and keeps a tracked changes version of the plan. It then consults on the main modifications for at least 6 weeks and sends the comments to the inspector.

The council can also make additional modifications: minor changes, such as corrections and updates, that don’t affect soundness.

You are a policy officer, Priya Shah, at the City of London Corporation, working with the rest of the policy team. You will only see the council’s screens. Where the inspector or a consultee would do something, a panel marked Prototype only does it for you.

The prototype starts after the consultation, with examples to look at: a version of the plan published for consultation and the comments made on it, and the team revising some modifications in the light of them, with modifications in draft, waiting for review and agreed. Use the menu on the left to move between pages in any order.
