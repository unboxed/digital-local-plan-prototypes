Digital Local Plans: Alpha prototypes

Welcome to the Digital Local Plans prototype site.

This project is funded by the Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government (MHCLG) and delivered by Unboxed and the City of London Corporation. We’ve developed and tested conceptual prototypes across the plan-making journey. These are based on the new 30 month plan-making process (opens in new tab).

You can try these out and share them with your colleagues. Please do not share them beyond your own team.

Before you start

  • these are highly conceptual prototypes – they do not represent what a live service will look like
  • we’ve tested ideas across different workflows, but these do not all join up (yet)
  • you’ll find lots of buttons and links that do not work – they are not broken, we just did not need them to work to test our ideas
