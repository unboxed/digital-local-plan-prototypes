Managing consultation responses

We learnt from our research that officers spend a lot of time manually sorting representations from consultees and members of the public, and then assigning a response to each individual representation on each issue. This prototype tests three ideas:

  • responding to common issues in bulk, by theme
  • better filtering and sorting, so officers and managers can check every representation has an appropriate response
  • seeing a consultee's history side by side, so the authority responds consistently
