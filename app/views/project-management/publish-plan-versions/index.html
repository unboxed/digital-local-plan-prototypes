Publish plan versions

An initial concept for publishing or sharing versions of your local plan. This prototype covers the option to:

  • publish the whole plan, images and associated layers and capture version details
  • view versions side by side
  • share chapters or a draft plan internally for non-public review
We're interested in learning about where this type of 'sharing' might happen within the drafting workflow.

Plan versions

As a policy officer: look back through the plan's versions, compare two side by side, and publish the current draft or share it internally.

Examination library

As an inspector, participant or member of the public: find the submitted plan, examination documents and evidence, and be sure each item is the version you should be looking at. Every item shows its reference, version and publication date, says if it has been superseded, and links to the examination view and GIS layers at the version that was examined.

Prototype only: in a real service this would be a public website, so it has its own header rather than this service's.
