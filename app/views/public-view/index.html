Public view

Prototypes exploring what members of the public see of a local plan, while it is being made and once it is adopted. Southampton is the example authority. These are for residents, businesses and community groups rather than LPA staff, so each one uses the council's own header without the site's internal navigation. Pick one to try it out.

Current prototypes

These are the most recent prototypes we've developed. We'll be testing these over the next few weeks.

A local plan being made

The council's local plan page during plan-making: a timeline of the key milestones, what you can do now, and a plain English explanation of what a local plan is.

User need

As a member of the public I need to know where the local plan is up to and when I can have my say, so that I can influence the planning policy for where I live

An adopted local plan

A front door to an adopted plan, modelled on the GOV.UK homepage. Search for a topic, enter a postcode or draw an area on a map to find the chapters that matter to you. Chapter pages are not built yet. There are two versions to compare:

  • Search-led: a big search box first, with place search and chapters below
  • Map-led: a map first, with results that update as you draw

Searching for ‘affordable housing’ finds an example policy page: the summary and full policy text, with the map, evidence, consultation and examination alongside.

User need

As a member of the public I need to find the policies in the local plan that apply to a topic or a place I care about, so that I can understand what can be built there
