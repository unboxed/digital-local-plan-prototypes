What is a local plan?

A local plan sets out how land in Southampton can be used and developed. It says how many new homes we need and where they should go, which places should be protected, and what new buildings should look like.

Once it is adopted, the council must use it to decide planning applications. That includes large developments, and changes to homes and businesses near you.

Why it matters to you

The local plan affects things like:

  • where new homes are built, and how many are affordable
  • which parks, green spaces and wildlife areas are protected
  • how tall new buildings can be
  • where schools, health centres and shops go
  • how the city prepares for flooding and climate change

How you can get involved

There are several points where you can tell us what you think. The timeline above shows when they are.

  • Consultation on the proposed plan content and evidence – happening now. Tell us what you think of our vision, where development should go and the evidence behind it.
  • Consultation on the proposed local plan – in 2027. Comment on the near-final plan before it is sent for examination.
  • Examination – an independent inspector checks the plan. People who commented on the proposed local plan may be invited to speak at hearings.

You don't need to know about planning to take part. Tell us about the places you know, in your own words.

What happens to your comments

We read every comment. We publish a summary of what people told us and explain how it changed the plan, or why it didn't. Your name may be published with your comment, but your address and contact details will not be.
