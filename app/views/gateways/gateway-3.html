Gateway 3 process

At Gateway 3 the plan is submitted for independent examination. It goes with a statement of compliance showing how it meets the requirements prescribed by regulation 32 of the Town and Country Planning (Local Planning) (England) Regulations 2026 (opens in a new tab), and the evidence for each.

Completed statement of compliance

Every requirement fully met, with no further action required: the statement as it would be submitted with the plan for independent examination.

Statement of general conformity with the London Plan

Part of the evidence for regulation 32(j): the Greater London Authority’s representations on whether the plan is in general conformity with the spatial development strategy (the London Plan, in this example), and an optional short statement from the authority.
